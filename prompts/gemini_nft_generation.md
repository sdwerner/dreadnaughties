# Prompt for Gemini Pro Image Generation
**Context for the AI:**
I am building a Web3 game called "Dreadnaughties". The game is a minimalist, math-heavy space combat simulator running on Bayesian probability mechanics. However, to contrast the complex math, the visual aesthetic should be distinctly "cute, minimalist, and slightly chunky"—think of "Battleships" but designed like modern, premium designer toys or soft minimalist UI assets.

**Task:**
Please generate a cohesive set of 4 visual concepts for our MVP Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs). 
The style should be: Isometric or flat 2D, soft color palettes (pastel blues, vibrant reds, sleek greys), clean vector-like shading, and no harsh black outlines.

Please generate the following 4 assets individually:

### Asset 1: The Base Hull (ERC-721)
**Description:** A "Dreadnaughtie" base ship. 
**Details:** It should look like a cute, chunky, futuristic spaceship chassis. It needs to have clearly visible "empty slots" or recessed compartments on its back/deck where upgrades could snap into place (like Lego). It should look sturdy and defensive, primarily in a metallic grey or calm ocean blue.

### Asset 2: The Alpha Component - "The Glass Cannon" (ERC-1155)
**Description:** A weapon system that increases attack probability (Red Balls/Alpha).
**Details:** This should be an oversized, cute, slightly menacing but cartoonish laser cannon or battery. It should clearly be designed to "snap" into one of the slots on the Base Hull. Its dominant color must involve **Vibrant Red** (to match the "Red Ball" math mechanic). It should look aggressive but not hyper-realistic.

### Asset 3: The Beta Component - "The Turtle Shell" (ERC-1155)
**Description:** A defensive armor or shield generator that increases survival/yield probability (Blue Balls/Beta).
**Details:** This should look like a heavy, glowing energy dome projector or a chunky piece of physical ablative armor. It also needs to look like it snaps into a hull slot. Its dominant color must involve **Deep Blue** (to match the "Blue Ball" math mechanic). It should look heavy, safe, and fortified.

### Asset 4: The Open Sea Staking UI Concept (Mockup)
**Description:** A conceptual scene of the "Open Sea".
**Details:** Show a top-down or isometric view of several "Dreadnaughtie" hulls floating peacefully in a minimalist, grid-like digital "ocean". The ships that have more Blue "Turtle Shell" components should have a subtle, glowing blue aura around them, indicating they are generating high passive yield. The water/grid should feel calm and lucrative.
